# Direct product copy rewrite

## Goal

Rewrite all user-facing FishTankr copy so it sounds like a knowledgeable mate checking the setup: brief, practical, supportive and clear.

## What will change

- Tighten page titles, headings, introductions, buttons, labels, empty states, warnings and confirmation messages across every content page.
- Replace long instructional paragraphs with short, scannable lines, especially in the tank builder, 3D editor, scorecard and pre-stock check.
- Simplify tooltips and accessibility labels while keeping them specific.
- Cut repeated disclaimers and explanations from everyday screens. Keep essential welfare, legal, privacy and terms meaning intact.
- Remove vague, corporate and AI-sounding language, including the banned terms in the brief.
- Use Australian English, sentence case and no em dashes.
- Keep scoring rules, species facts, database content, 3D behaviour and app functionality unchanged.

## Copy rules

- Lead with the action or problem.
- One point per sentence.
- Prefer labels and short bullets over paragraphs.
- Say what needs changing and why, without lecturing.
- Keep fish welfare warnings firm but calm.
- Do not overstate what the score can prove.

## Verification

- Search the project for banned terms, em dashes and known verbose phrases.
- Check the builder, scorecard, species pages, guides, quiz, shops, saved tanks, shared tanks and policy pages at mobile and desktop sizes.
- Confirm every content page still has complete, unique page metadata.
- Run the relevant tests and confirm the app loads without console errors.
